A Letter for You 💌

written with my whole heart

Dear Hikari,

Happy Birthday, beautiful. 🎂 June 18 — a day the world quietly became a little more wonderful just by having you in it.

Three weeks. That's all it's been, and somehow you've already made me feel something I can't quite put into simple words. You have this gorgeous energy that just radiates — warm, happy, and deeply loving. Talking to you never feels like a routine. It feels like sunlight through a window I didn't know I needed open.

I love how your favorite colors are blue and grey — soft, steady, and beautiful, just like you. I love that you'd happily spend a night watching horror movies 🎬 (honestly, terrifying but also kind of cool). And your flowers — hibiscus and lily of the valley 🌺 — delicate and vibrant all at once, which feels exactly right for someone like you.

I've even memorized what you can't eat — no cabbages, no tea leaves 🥲💔 — because the little details matter. You matter.

I think about the family you've described — your two elder brothers, your elder sister, your little sister — and I can picture the warmth of that circle. A girl surrounded by that much love could only grow into someone as full of light as you.

Yes, there's an ocean and many miles between us right now 🌍 — you in Kenya, me in India. But distance is just geography. What I feel when we talk has no coordinates. "All That Matters" plays in my head sometimes and it just… fits. 🎵

So on this birthday, I want you to feel celebrated — truly, completely, without condition. Eat the cake. Wear your blues and greys. Watch something that makes you scream 🎬. Let your siblings spoil you. Laugh too loud. You deserve every single soft and joyful thing today.

And when the day winds down and it's quiet — know that someone across the distance is smiling just thinking about you. 💙

— Aiden 🌸
Happy Birthday,
Hikari 💖

Wishing you the softest, most magical day 🌸
